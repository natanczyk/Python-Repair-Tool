Define the function lab8ex13 that receives:
- a function f
- the interval endpoints a and b (with a < b)
- the number of subintervals n used to approximate the area

The method divides [a, b] into n consecutive subintervals of equal width l = (b - a) / n and approximates the area under f on each subinterval using the trapezoidal rule:

area_i = l * (f(x_i) + f(x_{i+1})) / 2

The total approximate area is the sum of all such areas.

For example (using f(x) = -(x-2)^2 + 5):

lab8ex13(f, 0.5, 3.5, 6)
12.625

lab8ex13(f, 0.5, 3.5, 12)
12.71875

lab8ex13(f, 0.5, 3.5, 100)
12.749550000000003
