Write the function lab9ex10a that receives two integers n (numerator) and d (denominator) and returns a rational number in canonical (reduced) form as a dictionary with keys 'n' and 'd'.

The canonical form is obtained by dividing both n and d by their GCD. The denominator must always be positive (if d is negative, move the sign to the numerator).

Raise ValueError if:
  - d is zero
  - n or d is not an integer

For example:

lab9ex10a(4, 6)
{'n': 2, 'd': 3}

lab9ex10a(3, 9)
{'n': 1, 'd': 3}

lab9ex10a(4, 0)   # raises ValueError
lab9ex10a(4.3, 2) # raises ValueError
