Consider any 3-digit integer such that the difference between its first and last digit is greater than 1.

Let this number be n.
Reverse the digits of n, obtaining ni.
Subtract the smaller from the larger, obtaining ns = |n - ni|.
Reverse the digits of ns, obtaining nsi.

You can verify that: ns + nsi = 1089

Write a function lab5ex10a that reproduces this mystery.
Your function should validate that the argument satisfies the conditions (3-digit integer with |first digit - last digit| > 1).

For example:

lab5ex10a(246)
1089

lab5ex10a(131)
'Conditions not satisfied'
