Write the function lab5ex8 that receives:
- an integer r
- a non-negative integer n

and returns the sum of the first n+1 terms of the geometric series:

1 + r + r^2 + r^3 + ... + r^n

That is: sum of r^i for i = 0 to n.

For example:

lab5ex8(2, 4)
31

lab5ex8(100, 0)
1

lab5ex8(100, -1)
ValueError: lab5ex8: invalid argument
