Write the function lab8ex7 that receives:
- a matrix of dimension m×n
- a matrix of dimension n×r

and returns a matrix corresponding to the product of the two matrices.

If A and B are the matrices being multiplied, the elements of the resulting matrix are:

pij = sum over k of (Aik * Bkj)

For example:

lab8ex7([[1, 2], [3, 4]], [[5, 6], [7, 8]])
[[19, 22], [43, 50]]
