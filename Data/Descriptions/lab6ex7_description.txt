Two words of equal length are called "friendly" if the number of positions in which the corresponding characters differ is less than 10% of the word length.

Write the predicate lab6ex7 that receives two strings as arguments and returns True if the arguments correspond to friendly words and False otherwise. Words of different lengths are not friendly.

For example:

lab6ex7('amigas', 'amigas')
True

lab6ex7('amigas', 'asigos')
False
