Write the function lab9ex12 that receives a dictionary mapping paper identifiers (strings) to citation counts (integers), and returns the h-index of the researcher.

The h-index is defined as the largest value h such that at least h papers have at least h citations each.

Algorithm: sort the citation counts in descending order. The h-index is the largest h where the h-th paper (1-indexed) has at least h citations.

For example:

pubs = {'A': 10, 'B': 5, 'C': 4, 'D': 3, 'E': 8}
lab9ex12(pubs)
4

Explanation: sorted citations = [10, 8, 5, 4, 3].
h=4: the 4 most cited papers have [10, 8, 5, 4] citations, all >= 4. Valid.
h=5: only 3 papers have >= 5 citations. Invalid.
So h-index = 4.

lab9ex12({})
0
