Write the function lab9ex11 that receives a chess board represented as a dictionary, a queen's position, and the queen's color, and returns all pieces the queen can attack.

Parameters:
  - board: dict mapping (row, col) tuples to [piece_type, color] lists.
            Rows and columns are integers from 1 to 8.
  - queen_pos: (row, col) tuple with the queen's position (not in the board dict)
  - queen_color: 'white' or 'black'

The queen moves in 8 directions (horizontal, vertical, diagonal). In each direction it stops at the first piece encountered. It can only attack pieces of the opposite color.

Return a dictionary with two keys:
  - 'white': list of white pieces attacked (when queen is black)
  - 'black': list of black pieces attacked (when queen is white)

Each attacked piece is represented as [piece_type, color, (row, col)].

For example:

board = {(4, 7): ['pawn', 'black']}
lab9ex11(board, (4, 4), 'white')
{'white': [], 'black': [['pawn', 'black', (4, 7)]]}
