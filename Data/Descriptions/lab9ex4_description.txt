Write the function lab9ex4 that receives a dictionary 'notas' mapping grade values (integers 0-20) to lists of student numbers that received that grade, and returns a tuple (avg, failed) where:
  - avg: the average grade of students who passed (grade >= 10), as a float
  - failed: the total count of students who failed (grade < 10)

If no student passed, avg should be 0.0.

For example:

notas = {1: [46592, 49212, 90300, 59312], 15: [52592, 59212], 20: [58323]}
lab9ex4(notas)
(50/3, 4)

Explanation: Students with grades 15 and 20 passed (avg = (15+15+20)/3 = 50/3).
Students with grade 1 failed (count = 4).
